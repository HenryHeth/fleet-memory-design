# Across all types · How do we know it's working?

**Memory type:** All

## What we're seeing

Our early scorecard rated features and was wrong. We want measured, automatic scores that keep running.

## Our current position

- Plant made-up canaries; probe each memory part in fresh sessions at 10 minutes, a day, a week and a month, with no hint to look things up.
- Headline number: the **should-have-known rate**, found by scanning real chats, split by memory type.
- Alert only when a score drops.

## Questions for you

2. Is the should-have-known rate the right headline number? What would you track instead?
3. What would you add to, or cut from, the test matrix?
